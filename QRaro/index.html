FAQ

Everything you need to know

Can I scan a QR code from my phone camera?

Yes. The camera scanner uses the browser camera API and can decode QR codes live in supported devices.

What file types are supported?

For upload and decoding, QRora supports PNG, JPG, JPEG, and WEBP files.

Can I download a high-resolution QR?

Yes. You can export PNG, SVG, or PDF with a resolution and layout optimized for scannability.

What if my image is too large for a QR code?

Standard QR codes have limited data capacity. For large images, QRora warns you and recommends a shareable reference or hosted file approach.
